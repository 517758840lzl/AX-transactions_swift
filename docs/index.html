LIGACAO VITAL - COMERCIO E SERVICOS, (SU), LDA ("we") operates SpendLogBook ("this app"). This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app.

This app is a bookkeeping tool that runs on your device. The nickname, accounts, transactions, budgets, ideas and other records you enter stay in this app's private storage on that device. They are not uploaded to us. If you save the book to iCloud, one copy is placed in your own iCloud, not on a server we operate. This app does not provide user accounts or a bank connection, and it does not include advertising or usage-statistics software.

This policy describes the following:

1. Information kept on the device

The ledger is written only from what you enter, import or clear in this app:

  • Profile: nickname and gender. Gender may be left undisclosed. A nickname is not required in order to use the app. The mark beside the nickname is drawn from that gender. It is not a photograph.
  • Preferences: interface language, currency, and whether amounts are hidden.
  • Accounts: name, opening balance, account type, whether the account counts toward net worth, whether it is archived, and, for a card, the statement day and due day you set.
  • Transactions: name, amount, direction, category, date, account, whether it has been checked, whether it is marked for the household view, whether it counts in the budget, reimbursement status, refund link, saving-goal link, and a link between the two halves of one purchase.
  • Budgets for the month and for categories, category names, saving goals, repeating charges, and items in Recently deleted.
  • Ideas: the words you write, the kind you choose, tags, whether you mark one as a favorite, the date, and a picture you attach. That picture stays on the device.
  • The contents of a JSON backup that you choose to import. Importing replaces the ledger on that device.

We do not receive your telephone number, email address, identity document number, contacts, precise location, advertising identifiers or a device identifier for tracking. A picture stays in the book only when you attach it to an idea. This app does not offer registration, sign-in, or sign-in through a third-party account.

2. Purposes of use

The records above are used only to provide the functions on that device: recording income and expenditure, showing balances and net worth, filtering by time and category, calculating a remaining budget, showing a year report and saving goals, keeping ideas, and moving money between your own accounts.

The household mark is a viewing scope inside the ledger on that device. It does not send records to any other person or device. A rank shown from days you have logged is calculated on the device from those dates. It is not a score sent anywhere.

We do not use these records for advertising, profiling, credit assessment or automated decision-making, and we do not sell the ledger.

3. Pictures you attach

An idea can hold one picture. You choose it in the system picker. The app keeps only that picture, and only on this device. It does not receive permission to browse the photo library, and it does not send the picture to us.

The picture is included if you export a JSON backup or save the book to iCloud. An Excel export does not include it. Removing the picture from the idea, or clearing the book, deletes it from this device.

4. On-device reminders

If you keep an active card and set a statement day, this app may ask for permission to show notifications. If you allow it, the app schedules reminders on the device: one on the statement morning, and another on the due-day morning when that day is different. Both are set for 9:00 in the device's local time. The text can include the card name and the amount for that period, or *** when amounts are hidden.

These reminders are created by the system on your device. They are not sent to us, and we do not operate a push service. If you refuse permission, archive or delete the card, or clear the ledger, the pending card reminders from this app are removed.

A daily reminder is off until you turn it on. You choose the time. If you allow notifications, the app schedules one local reminder at that time. The text is a prompt to record, not the contents of a transaction. Turning the reminder off, or clearing the ledger's reminder setting, removes that pending reminder. We do not send it from a server.

5. Storage and retention

The ledger, including idea pictures, is stored in this app's private folder on the device. Uninstalling the app deletes that folder's contents. Clear data, under Data in Me, removes accounts, transactions, budgets, repeating charges, saving goals, ideas and their pictures, and recently deleted items. Nickname, gender, language, currency, the hidden-amount setting and category names are kept.

How long the ledger remains is up to you. Until you clear it or uninstall the app, it stays on the device so you can continue it the next time you open the app.

We do not keep a copy of our own. A copy can exist in your iCloud, as section 6 describes. We cannot look it up, restore it or delete it for you.

6. iCloud

iCloud here means the Apple ID already signed in on the phone. This app does not ask you to create an account with us, and it does not receive the Apple ID, password or the contents of the copy.

When iCloud is available and the book contains records you entered, the app writes one file into this app's iCloud container. That can happen when you tap Save to iCloud, when you next open the app, or shortly after you change such a book. The file is the book itself, including idea pictures. An empty book is not written up. Clearing the book on the phone does not delete a copy already in iCloud. A later save replaces that copy only after the book on the phone again has records you entered.

Bring back replaces the book on this phone with the iCloud file, and only after you confirm. Apple stores that file under the iCloud terms for the signed-in Apple ID. We do not operate that storage and cannot open it.

7. Export, import and sharing

We do not operate a server that receives the ledger, and we do not provide it to advertisers, data brokers or a bank interface. Balances and budgets are calculated on the device.

Export can write two kinds of file, and then opens the system share sheet. A JSON backup is the book, including ideas and any picture attached to an idea. An Excel spreadsheet has three sheets: transactions, accounts and budgets. It does not include ideas or pictures. The file goes only where you send it, such as Files, Mail or AirDrop. We do not receive that file.

Import reads a file you select and replaces the ledger on the device. If the file cannot be read as a ledger, the current ledger is left as it is.

If you back up the device, the operating system and the backup service you choose may include this app's private folder. We do not operate that backup and cannot inspect it. If the device is lost and no backup remains under your control, we cannot restore the ledger.

8. Software included in the app

The app includes software that draws charts and plays the opening animation. Both run on the device. They do not receive the ledger, do not contact us, and do not include advertising or measurement of how you use the app.

9. Your management of the information

  • Access and correction: open the account, transaction, nickname, budget, idea or other record and change it. A picture on an idea can be removed there.
  • Deletion of the ledger: Clear data removes the items listed in section 5. It does not delete a copy already in iCloud.
  • iCloud copy: a later save replaces it after the book again has records you entered. This app has no separate control that deletes only the iCloud file.
  • Notifications: you can refuse the permission request, or turn notifications off for this app in the system settings.
  • Uninstallation: uninstalling the app deletes the ledger stored in the app's private folder. A copy already in iCloud can remain there.

This app has no account server. There is no remote copy for us to retrieve or delete.

10. Children

This app is intended for people who keep their own accounts. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Please do not record a child's identity document number or other sensitive identifying information in the ledger.

A parent or guardian may change or clear the ledger on the device, or uninstall the app. We do not hold a server copy that could be deleted from our side.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy is revised, the revised text is published on this page, and the date at the top is updated. That date is the publication date and the effective date of that version. From that date, the revised text replaces the previous version. The same text is shown in the app under Me, Privacy.

This app does not keep user accounts or a list of email addresses, so we cannot send a separate notice of a change. Publication on this page is the notice. Continuing to use the app after the date at the top means you have read the version with that date.
